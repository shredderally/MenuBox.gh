FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

Answers below are the same text used in the FAQPage structured data on this page.

How much does MenuBoxGh cost in Ghana?

Standard is GHS 3,500 setup plus GHS 500 per month. Premium is GHS 4,500 setup plus GHS 600 per month. Custom pricing is available on request. Confirm current pricing on WhatsApp before paying.

Do customers need to download an app to order?

No. Customers scan a QR code, browse the menu in their phone browser, and send the order through WhatsApp.

How does WhatsApp ordering work?

The customer selects items, table number or delivery details, and the order is sent as a structured WhatsApp message with customer, table, items, total and payment status.

What payment methods are supported?

Standard includes manual MoMo payment. Premium adds Paystack for MoMo, card and bank payments. Confirm any payment-provider fees separately.

How long does setup take?

MenuBoxGh states setup in 72 hours. Confirm scope and timing on WhatsApp before committing to a launch date.

Can I change prices and availability?

Yes. The site states you can change prices or availability quickly. Confirm who makes changes — you or the MenuBoxGh team — during onboarding.
